SOUND CLIPS GO HERE
==================
Use .ogg files encoded as Ogg Vorbis. Use mono for positional world sounds.
Do not just rename WAV/MP3 files; export or convert them to Ogg Vorbis.

Example: portal/open.ogg in this folder is referenced as naturality:portal/open
in resources/sounds.json. See resources/README.txt for a complete example that
replaces the existing portal opening sound. All clips here are packaged by Gradle.
