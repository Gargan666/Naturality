- NATURALITY -
The Minecraft I envisioned

Naturality is a mod aiming to lean into the "pixel" aesthetic of minecraft, overhauling and changing many systems to better fit the style.
Changes are mostly intended for singleplayer, but I'll try my best to make all serverside changes toggleable.
I cannot guarantee that the mod works with other mods (especially visual mods) perfectly.

- FEATURES -

- Fluids -

Both lava and water share a bunch of visual overhauls, many being inspired by and similar to mods you may have seen before!


Dynamic textures

By recreating the actual mathematical formulas used to create vanilla fluid textures, this mod renders them in real time with randomization, getting rid of that clear tiling present in vanilla textures! Intended to look as similar as possible to vanilla textures.
