CLUSTERED RAIN AND SNOW
======================
Replacement artwork:
  resources/textures/particle/rain_cluster.png, rain_cluster_1.png, rain_cluster_2.png
  resources/textures/particle/snow_cluster.png, snow_cluster_1.png, snow_cluster_2.png

These are temporary, transparent 128x128 PNGs. Each contains 25-50 marks scattered
across one square card. Replace any of these files with your artwork, rebuild, and reload
resources/restart the client. Use a transparent background, leave a clear margin,
and draw rain streaks vertically. Snow uses dots/small flakes. White/grayscale is
recommended: world light applies to both, with biome water color tinting on rain. Other square
resolutions work; resolution does not change the card's 3.6-4.6 block width.
Particle JSON files under src/client/resources/assets/naturality/particles select
random image variants if more textures are listed. They are not animation strips.

Each cluster is one simulated particle and one quad, rather than 25 independently
simulated droplets. Sprite Y stays parallel to velocity. Cards rotate around that axis toward the viewer position, independently of camera rotation.
Rain streaks follow the actual falling direction; each card has a 50% horizontal
mirror chance.
Wind tilts the trajectory. Snow falls about ten times slower, with horizontal sway and gentle fall-speed changes.
Particles stay at world positions as the camera moves. They fade on birth/expiry,
near the camera, at the edge of the effect and approaching surfaces.

Natural weather has at most 1536 tracked cluster particles and at most 80 births
from 320 spawn attempts per tick. A shared particle-engine limit is 1600 including
manually spawned cards. At weather-radius setting 10, the actual radius is 15 blocks.
Target counts: rain 25 = 70, rain 50 = 141, rain 75 = 193, rain 100 = 563.
Density grows cubically above 50. Radius is 1.5 times the setting, capped at 36 blocks.
Decreased particles halves the budget; Minimal quarters it rather than removing
weather entirely. Existing particle-engine limits/eviction remain in force.

Loaded heightmaps are checked across each card's entire conservative footprint;
no chunks are loaded. Cards terminate before entering roofs, ground or water.
This intentionally fades a whole cluster near uneven terrain instead of simulating
individual droplet collisions; a small surface clearance can be visible. Vanilla
rain impacts are thinned by (rain/100)^2: 1% retained at 10, 6.25% at 25,
25% at 50 and 100% at 100. Sounds, water/lava rings and server snow/ice remain.
Biome and temperature queries choose rain/snow; dry biomes produce neither. Existing
cards retire on biome/type changes, clear weather, disabling, distance/lifetime
expiry, dimension changes or particle-engine resource reload eviction.

The vanilla precipitation sheet pass is skipped only while Naturality weather is
active in a weather-capable dimension. Disabled profiles and unmodded servers keep
the vanilla path. Nether and End remain disabled. Both classic and improved
transparency use Minecraft's translucent particle rendering and local light.

Implementation: ParticleWeather, WeatherClusterParticle, WeatherRendererMixin;
RAIN_CLUSTER/SNOW_CLUSTER registrations and rain_cluster/snow_cluster particle JSON.
Verification: gradlew assemble; gradlew runClientGameTest
  -PclientTests=particle-weather,weather
The focused particle suite checks budgets, replacement of sheets, position-facing
orientation including head-on views, slow/swaying snow, roofs, dry biomes, clear
weather and dimension cleanup, with screenshots in both transparency modes.




The velocity constraint applies to snow too: whole-card in-plane spin is removed
because it rotates sprite Y away from velocity. Exact head-on views use a stable
perpendicular fallback normal without releasing the axis lock.
